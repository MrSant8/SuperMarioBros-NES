Super Mario Bros NES

UPC - Remake Project – 2025

🎮 Description

Super Mario Bros is a plataform video game desenvoluped by Nintendo in 1985. In it, Mario and his brother Lugi have to rescue Princess Peach, who has been kidnapped by Bowser, the Koopas' king. To do so, they must journey through eight worlds filled with enemies, obstacles, and hidden shortcuts. Each level has different enemies, you can find coins, teleportation zones "Warp Zones" and the flagpole at the end, where the higher you jump the more points you earn. Players can collect ítems to aid them in their mission: the Super Mushroom increases Mario's size and durability, the Fire Flower allows him to throw fireballs, and the Starman grants temporary invincibility.Throughout the game, players face a impostor Bowser in each castle before reaching the final showdown with Bowser. After completing the game, players can restart it to experience an increase in difficulty, featuring faster enemies and level changes, providing a more difficult gameplay experience.

💥 Power-Ups

Super Mushroom

Makes Mario bigger and stronger.

Fire Flower

Lets Mario shoot fireballs.

Starman

Grants invincibility for a few seconds.

🧱 Interactable Objects

Brick Block

Can be broken by Super Mario or contain hidden items.

Question Block

Contains coins or power-ups. Activated by hitting it from below.

Flagpole

Marks the end of the level. The higher you reach, the more points you get.

Warp Pipe

Used to transport Mario between areas or to hidden levels.

Coin

Collect 100 coins to earn an extra life.
